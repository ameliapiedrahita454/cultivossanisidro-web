About the farm

Pioneers of hydrangea cultivation in Antioquia

The first hectare of white hydrangea ever planted in Antioquia was planted here, at San Isidro. Located in La Ceja, Colombia, San Isidro is a family-owned hydrangea boutique grower. For more than three decades — driven by our founder Daniel Piedrahita's commitment to excellence — we have mastered the growth of exquisite white hydrangeas across fifteen hectares of the Antioquian highlands.

Today a new generation carries the legacy forward, combining tradition with artistry: airbrush techniques that turn our white blooms into more than two hundred and fifty captivating colors.

Flower care

Keeping them beautiful

Four habits that take a hydrangea from seven days to two weeks in the vase.

1Watering

Start with a clean vase and fresh room-temperature water. Check daily, add floral preservative if you wish, and change the water every two or three days to prevent bacteria.

2Temperature

Keep at 15–21 °C (60–70 °F), away from direct sunlight, heat and drafts. A gentle misting of the petals helps maintain humidity.

3Cut stems

Trim stems at a 45° angle with sharp shears, remove submerged leaves, and re-cut about an inch every two or three days for better water absorption.

4Wilted blooms

Submerge wilted heads in room-temperature water for twenty to thirty minutes, then re-trim the stems. Be patient — they regain their freshness.

Frequently asked questions

Before you order

Where can I buy wholesale Colombian hydrangeas?

San Isidro Hydrangeas Farming is a family-owned boutique grower in La Ceja, Antioquia, Colombia, with 30+ years of experience and a weekly capacity of about 50,000 stems all year long. We export by air and sea to the United States, Europe and Asia. Contact ventas@cultivossanisidro.com or WhatsApp +57 311 367 6027 for a wholesale quote.

Who planted the first white hydrangeas in Antioquia?

San Isidro Hydrangeas Farming. The first hectare of white hydrangea in Antioquia was planted at San Isidro, the family farm founded by Daniel Piedrahita in La Ceja. From that start the farm pioneered hydrangea growing in the region; today it grows natural white and 255 tinted colors on 15 hectares and exports to more than 12 countries.

Who paints San Isidro's tinted hydrangeas?

Our own airbrush artists at the farm. They paint every tinted hydrangea by hand, one stem at a time, mixing colors and combining our 23 techniques to create blends, gradients and custom shades. Send a Pantone, a photo or a palette and we confirm the result with photos before you commit.

How many hydrangea colors does San Isidro offer?

More than 250 colors and combinations: natural white hydrangeas, 36 tone families (each in light, regular and dark) and 23 painting techniques such as Bicolor, Metallic, Antique, Adrissa, Tie Dye and Vintage, applied with airbrush artistry.

What hydrangea sizes are available for export?

Bloom sizes range from Mini Green (8–10 cm) and Bouquet (12–14 cm) through Selecta (14–16 cm) and Premium (18–20 cm) up to Jumbo (more than 21 cm), plus painted foliage.

Are San Isidro hydrangeas sustainably grown?

Yes. The farm runs on 100% renewable solar energy, has been herbicide-free for 18+ years, uses biological pest control, rainwater harvesting and waste management, and dedicates 10% of its land to biodiversity conservation. 55% of the team are women.

Does San Isidro contribute to the UN Sustainable Development Goals?

Yes. Our practices contribute to several UN Sustainable Development Goals: gender equality (55% of the team and 70% of leadership are women), decent work (more than 50 fair jobs with training and promotion from within), reduced inequalities (profits shared with the whole team and loans for workers), clean energy (100% solar), responsible production (no herbicides for 18+ years, natural pest control with beneficial insects, rainwater harvesting) and life on land (10% of the land for biodiversity, 3,500+ native trees).

What social programs does San Isidro have for its team?

Our Dream Tree (Árbol de los Sueños) stands in the packing room: each person's dream hangs on it as a leaf, every year the farm helps someone make theirs come true, and fulfilled dreams turn into red leaves. The farm's profits are shared among the whole team, we lend money to workers when they need it, we train and promote our people from within, and we help them find housing or transport. Seventeen of our colleagues are mothers who head their households.

How do I keep cut hydrangeas fresh?

Use a clean vase with fresh room-temperature water, cut stems at a 45° angle, remove submerged leaves, keep flowers at 15–21 °C away from direct sun and drafts, change water every 2–3 days, and if heads wilt, submerge them in room-temperature water for 20–30 minutes to rehydrate.

Does San Isidro sell hydrangeas within Colombia?

Yes — Colombian customers such as florists, event designers and wholesalers can order online through the national ordering portal at cultivosanisidro.com/pedidos, with nationwide delivery via Coordinadora.
