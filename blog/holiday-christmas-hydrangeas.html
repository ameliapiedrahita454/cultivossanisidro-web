Frequently asked questions

Which hydrangea colors sell best at Christmas?

Deep red, gold and natural white with silver. Our catalog includes tinted reds and Christmas techniques such as Rainbow Christmas, Picasso Christmas, Tie Dye Christmas and Poinsettia.

Do tinted hydrangeas last through the holidays?

Stems are cut and tinted the same day, and with good handling they last 7 to 15 days in the vase.
