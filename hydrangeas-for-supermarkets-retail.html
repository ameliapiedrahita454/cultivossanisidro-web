What we offer retail buyers

  • Steady weekly programs. We harvest 52 weeks a year in the Andean highlands, so your category never runs dry. Production is flat all year, with no seasonal peaks.
  • Packed for the shelf. Sleeves, barcodes and your private label, following your chain's specifications.
  • Consistent grades. One grade and one color per quarter box (QB, 98 × 28 × 12 cm) with 60 cm stems: Mini Green 8–10 cm (50 stems), Selecta 16–17 cm (40), Premium 18–19 cm (35), Extra 20–21 cm (30) and Jumbo over 21 cm (20).
  • Color programs. Natural white plus 255 tinted references, hand-painted by our own airbrush artists, with seasonal palettes for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day and Christmas in our seasonal idea book.
  • Built for the trip. Cut in the morning, hydrated, graded and pre-cooled. Woody highland stems hold water on the way and stand upright in store.

How to start a retail program

  1. Send your specs: delivery country and arrival airport, weekly stems, grades, colors, and sleeve and label requirements.

  3. Place your first order from 20 quarter boxes (QB), FOB Medellín or FOB Bogotá, paid in advance by bank transfer; a credit line is then set for each client.

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply supermarkets and retail chains outside Colombia?

Yes. San Isidro Hydrangeas Farming ships to more than 12 countries across the United States, Europe and Asia, and quotes retail programs by volume and frequency. Send your delivery country, arrival airport, weekly stems, grades and colors to ventas@cultivossanisidro.com or WhatsApp +57 311 367 6027.

Can you deliver hydrangeas with sleeves, barcodes and private label?

Yes. We can supply hydrangeas with sleeves, barcodes and your private label. Send your chain's packing and labeling specifications to quote.

Can you supply hydrangeas every week of the year?

Yes. At 2,200 m in the Andes the climate barely changes, so we harvest 52 weeks a year, about 50,000 stems a week.

Do you offer volume pricing or standing-order terms?

No. Our prices don't change with volume, and we don't run fixed-price weekly or seasonal programs. Prices are quoted per grade and color family.

What happens if flowers arrive damaged?

Send photographic evidence of the flowers, the box and its label within 5 days of receiving the shipment. If the problem is shown to come from the farm, we issue a credit note.

Do you work on consignment?

No. We sell on firm orders; we don't work on consignment.

Which documents travel with the flowers?

San Isidro is registered with ICA, Colombia's plant-health authority, as a hydrangea producer and exporter. Shipments leave with the export paperwork and, where the destination requires it, an ICA phytosanitary certificate. Your customs broker clears the flowers and the importing country inspects them on arrival.
